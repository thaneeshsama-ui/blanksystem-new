Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

BlankSystem ("BlankSystem", "we", "us", or "our") operates the website blanksystem.in (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you interact with the Site, how we use it, and the choices available to you. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you choose to submit directly to us through the Site. Currently, this includes the fields available in our contact form:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Your message or project details

If we add further fields to our forms in the future (for example, phone number or company name), those fields will only be collected if you choose to provide them, and this policy will be updated to reflect any such changes. We do not currently operate any user account or login system, and we do not process any payments through the Site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information submitted through our contact form to:

  • Respond to your inquiry or request;
  • Discuss and provide the services you have asked about;
  • Communicate with you regarding your project, quote, or question;
  • Maintain records of our business communications.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for purposes unrelated to responding to and fulfilling your inquiry.

3. Contact Form Processing (Formspree)

Our contact form is processed using Formspree, a third-party form-handling service. When you submit the contact form, the information you enter is sent to Formspree's servers and then forwarded to us, typically by email. Formspree acts as a data processor on our behalf. We encourage you to review Formspree's own privacy policy to understand how they handle submitted data.

4. Newsletter Signup

The Site includes a newsletter subscription field in the footer. This newsletter signup form is not currently connected to any email marketing service or database, so any email address entered is not presently stored, processed, or used by us. Should we connect this form to a newsletter service in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy beforehand to explain how subscriber information will be collected, stored, and used, along with how you can unsubscribe.

5. Other Third-Party Service Providers

Beyond Formspree, information submitted to us may be stored or processed using general-purpose third-party tools we use to run our business, such as email hosting or productivity software. These providers only process information as necessary to help us operate and are not permitted to use it for their own independent purposes.

6. Cookies and Analytics

The Site does not currently use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics services. If we introduce analytics, cookies, or similar tracking technologies in the future, we will update this Privacy Policy to describe what is used, why, and what choices you have, in line with applicable requirements at that time.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect the information submitted to us, including relying on the security practices of the third-party services we use, such as Formspree. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of any information you submit to us.

8. Data Retention

We retain information submitted through the contact form for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, maintain records of our business communications, and comply with any applicable obligations. If you would like us to delete information you have previously sent us, you can contact us using the details below and we will address your request.

9. Third-Party Links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites, such as our social media profiles. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of any third-party sites, and we encourage you to review the privacy policy of any external site you visit.

10. Children's Privacy

The Site is intended for business inquiries and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding the personal information you have shared with us, such as requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of that information. We aim to honor reasonable requests of this kind for anyone who contacts us, regardless of location. To make such a request, please contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our practices or the Site, such as adding new form fields, connecting our newsletter, or introducing analytics. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us at:
